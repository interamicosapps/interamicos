About Us

Inter Amicos LLC is a technology company focused on developing intuitive mobile applications that make it easier for people to connect, meet up, and make plans with confidence.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create tools that make social coordination more transparent and less stressful. We focus on showing where activity is happening and when, so users can make informed decisions—whether they're planning ahead or deciding in the moment.

O State Nightlife

We're currently developing O State Nightlife, a mobile application designed for university communities. The app helps users see which bars and venues are active, where friends and other users plan to be later in the night, and how activity shifts over time.

Users can check in or indicate future plans within a time window, allowing others to view live and upcoming activity through a list and map-based interface. The goal is to help users avoid empty venues, coordinate meetups more easily, and discover where the energy is on any given night.

Our Approach

We combine clean, map-driven design with real-time data to surface useful insights at a glance. We prioritize user control, privacy, and clarity—giving people the information they want without making the experience feel invasive or overwhelming.
