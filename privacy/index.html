Privacy Policy

This is Inter Amicos LLC’s policy for Bar Fest. It is not legal advice.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Inter Amicos LLC (“we”, “us”) publishes the Bar Fest iOS app. This policy describes how Bar Fest handles information. Contact: iedwards@interamicos.com. We are based in Columbus, Ohio.

Who this app is for

Bar Fest is a nightlife app about bars, venues, deals, and local chat. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email iedwards@interamicos.com.

Information the app can collect

Anonymous ID

Bar Fest does not require an account, name, phone number, or email. The app creates a random identifier and stores it in the iOS Keychain on your device. That ID is used to associate your chat posts, location presence, and similar activity with your install of the app. It is not your Apple ID.

Location, only if you allow it

Bar Fest uses location to show nearby venues, crowd cues, and features that depend on being at or near a place.

  • While Using the App (or Allow Once): the app can run with location while it is in use. We may store a short-lived record that this anonymous ID was recently at a venue. The app uses those records to show recent activity. They are not treated as a live headcount.
  • Always: if you allow Always, the app may update venue presence while you are out, including in the background, so live headcounts can include you.

You can deny location or change it later in iOS Settings. If location is off, features that need it will be limited.

Chat

If you post, we store the message text, an anonymous author ID, an optional avatar (icon and color), and the time and thread the post belongs to. Other people using Bar Fest can see the post and avatar. They do not see your name or contact details from us.

Reports and optional submissions

You may report a chat post, send feedback about a venue, deal, or event, or submit a wait-time impression. We store what you submit, along with the anonymous ID when the app sends it, so we can operate and moderate the service.

Contact email

If you email iedwards@interamicos.com, we receive whatever you include in that message (your address, the text, and any attachments).

Technical data needed to run the service

Our backend receives the requests the app makes (for example to load venues, deals, events, chat, and presence). That can include standard server logs such as time, request type, and IP address.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not ask you to sign in with a social account. We do not use your contacts, photos, or microphone for Bar Fest.

How we use information

We use this information to operate Bar Fest: show the map and listings, show live and recent venue activity, deliver chat, review reports and feedback, fix problems, and answer email you send us.

How recent presence is used

Recent “while using” visits are for short-lived activity cues in the app. Live presence is for people who chose Always. We do not publish your anonymous ID as your identity. Crowd figures are counts, not a public profile of you.

Who we share information with

We use service providers to host and run the app, including Supabase for the database and API that store catalog content, chat, presence, and related submissions. Those providers process data for us to provide the service. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to protect the rights and safety of Inter Amicos LLC. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep information while we need it to operate Bar Fest, moderate content, and meet legal obligations. Recent presence is meant to reflect a short recent window and is not a permanent public history of your night. Chat posts remain until they are removed by moderation, by a deletion request we can fulfill, or when we delete them as part of operating the service. Email you send us is kept as needed to handle that conversation. The anonymous ID on your device stays until you delete the app or the Keychain item is removed.

Your choices

  • Location: iOS Settings → Bar Fest → Location.
  • Chat: you choose what to post. You can report posts in the app.
  • Deletion: email iedwards@interamicos.com with the subject “Bar Fest data deletion”. Tell us what you want removed. Because the app does not use an account login, we may need details you can still see in the app (for example the text of a post) to find the right records.
  • Uninstalling removes the on-device anonymous ID. It does not by itself erase data already stored on our servers.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to this app, including HTTPS and access controls on our backend. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of Bar Fest after the update means you accept the updated policy.
