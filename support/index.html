Bar Fest Support

Bar Fest is published by Inter Amicos LLC.

Bar Fest is a nightlife map for Columbus. You can browse venues, see deals and events, join local chat, and check how active a place looks. Live crowd counts depend on people who allow location in the background. Recent activity can appear from people who allow location only while the app is open.

Common questions

What is Bar Fest?

Bar Fest is a Columbus nightlife app from Inter Amicos LLC. It combines a venue map, deals, activities, local chat, and crowd cues so you can decide where to go.

How does location work?

Bar Fest asks for location only if you allow it.

If you choose Allow Once or While Using the App, you can use the app. The app may record that you were recently near a venue so others can see recent activity. That is not the same as a live headcount.

If you choose Always, Bar Fest can update your venue presence while you are out, including when the app is in the background. That presence can contribute to live headcounts.

You can change or turn off location anytime in iOS Settings → Bar Fest → Location.

What is the difference between live and recent?

Live headcounts reflect people who are sharing location with Always. Recent activity reflects people who were nearby with location allowed while using the app. A place can show recent activity without showing those same people as a live count.

Is chat anonymous?

Yes. Bar Fest does not ask you to create an account. Chat is tied to an anonymous ID stored on your device. Posts can show a chosen avatar (an icon and color). They do not show your name, phone number, or email.

How do I report a chat message or a bad listing?

In chat, use Report on the message. For a wrong venue, deal, event, or other listing problem, email iedwards@interamicos.com with the place name and what is wrong.

How do I ask to delete my data?

Email iedwards@interamicos.com with the subject “Bar Fest data deletion” and describe what you want removed. If you still have the app installed, say so. Uninstalling the app removes the anonymous ID stored on that device, but information already sent to our servers (such as chat posts or recent presence) is removed only if you ask us or we delete it in the normal course of operating the service.
